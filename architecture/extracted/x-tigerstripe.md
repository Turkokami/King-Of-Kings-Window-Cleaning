## Tiger striping — the vertical streaks on the gutter face

Almost every older gutter in this region has them: dark vertical stripes down the
outside face, evenly spaced, that will not wash off with soap and water.

They are not dirt in the ordinary sense. Runoff carrying dissolved material from
the roof — including from asphalt shingles — sheets over the gutter's front edge
in heavy rain, and the residue bonds to the oxidising painted aluminium surface.
Once it has bonded, general washing does nothing, which is why people conclude
their gutters are simply old.

They usually are not. [Gutter brightening](/services/gutter-cleaning/gutter-brightening/)
is a separate treatment that breaks the bond and takes the striping off, and it
is one of the highest-visual-return jobs on a house — it makes a roofline look
maintained in a way that is out of proportion to what it costs.
