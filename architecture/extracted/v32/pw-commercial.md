## Commercial work

Storefront entrances, walkways, loading areas, parking structures, refuse areas
and shared hardstanding on HOA and multi-unit properties. Usually scheduled,
usually early or after-hours, and usually driven by the same slip-and-liability
consideration described above rather than by appearance.

For commercial clients the runoff question is not optional — it is a compliance
matter, and a contractor who cannot explain their approach to it is a risk to
carry.
