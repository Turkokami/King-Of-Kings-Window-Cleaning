## Between washes

Four things extend the interval, and none of them are products.

**Cut the planting back from the wall.** The single highest-return change. Air
movement against siding is what dries it, and a shrub pressed against the wall
prevents that permanently.

**Trim overhanging branches.** Less shade, less drip, less organic debris landing
on and sticking to the elevation below.

**Fix the sprinkler heads that hit the house.** Daily wetting of one patch of
siding produces exactly the result you would expect, and it is free to correct.

**Keep the gutters clear.** Overflow staining down an elevation is a gutter
problem wearing a siding problem's clothes, and it will return every wet season
until the cause is dealt with.

What does not work: bleach in a garden sprayer aimed at a wall from the ground,
which mostly runs off, lands in the beds undiluted and streaks; and scrubbing
with a stiff brush, which on chalked vinyl or aged paint takes the finish with
the growth.
