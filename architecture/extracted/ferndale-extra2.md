## Where window cleaning meets the rest of the exterior

Windows are usually the first thing people notice and rarely the only thing
happening. On a Ferndale property, three connections come up over and over.

**Gutters above the glass.** A gutter that overflows in a Whatcom County downpour
does not spill neatly — it sheets down the wall and across the window below it,
carrying whatever was in the gutter with it. Cleaning glass under a blocked
gutter is a temporary measure. If we can see the overflow staining, we will point
at it, and you can decide whether to deal with the cause. See
[gutter cleaning in Ferndale](/locations/ferndale/gutter-cleaning/).

**Roof growth washing down.** Moss and algae on a roof shed a tea-coloured runoff
in heavy rain that stains everything below it, glass included. If your upper
windows stain faster than your lower ones, look up. See
[roof moss removal in Ferndale](/locations/ferndale/moss-removal/).

**Siding above and beside.** Chalky, oxidised vinyl siding sheds a white residue
onto glass every time it rains. Washing the windows without addressing the siding
means watching the residue come back on the first wet week. See
[house washing in Ferndale](/locations/ferndale/house-washing/).

We are not going to invent work that is not there. But we will tell you when the
thing you asked us to fix is downstream of something else, because the
alternative is you paying twice.

looking through it.

If something is not right, tell us then and we will fix it while we are still on
your driveway.

## The problems we get called about most

Most Ferndale calls are one of these:

- **Glass that never looks clean.** Nine times out of ten this is bonded film —
  industrial haze or dried field dust — not dirt, and it needs contact and
  detergent rather than a rinse.
- **White spotting that will not wash off.** Hard-water deposit. Treatable when
  it is fresh, permanent once it has etched.
- **Green or black in the frame corners.** Organic growth, driven by damp shade.
  Comes off, comes back, and comes back slower once the frames are properly
  cleared rather than just wiped.
- **Screens that make clean windows look dirty.** Extremely common and almost
  never diagnosed correctly. The glass is fine; you are looking through a dusty
  screen at it.
- **Something on the glass from a build.** Overspray, mortar, adhesive. Real risk
  of permanent scratching if it is approached the wrong way.
