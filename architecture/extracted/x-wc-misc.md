## Commercial and multi-unit work

Storefronts, offices, restaurants, medical and dental practices, apartment
blocks, HOA common areas and vacation rentals. Different rhythm from residential
work: scheduled rather than seasonal, usually monthly or quarterly, usually
early-morning or after-hours so a clean front does not mean a closed door.

What commercial clients tend to care about that homeowners do not: a consistent
crew who know the building, a schedule that is actually kept, an invoice that
matches the agreement, and someone who will not be on a ladder in front of
customers at eleven in the morning. Entry glass and anything at eye level
generally needs a much higher frequency than the rest of a building, and a
sensible schedule reflects that instead of treating all the glass the same.

Property managers and vacation-rental owners are a large part of this work, and
turnover cleans get their own scheduling because the window between guests is
the whole constraint.
