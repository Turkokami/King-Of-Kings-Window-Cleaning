## The glass in your house is not all the same

Worth knowing what you have, because it changes the method:

- **Annealed glass** — older single panes, soft and forgiving to clean, usually
  in wooden sashes that need care rather than force
- **Tempered glass** — stronger, safer, and carrying the latent surface debris
  described above
- **Low-E coated glass** — an invisible coating, generally on an inner surface of
  the sealed unit, that gives the pane a faint sheen people routinely mistake for
  a film that will not come off
- **Insulated glass units** — two or three panes with a sealed cavity; when the
  seal fails, the fog is inside and unreachable

Each of these has a [surface library](/surface-library/) entry going into what it
is, how it behaves and what it needs.
