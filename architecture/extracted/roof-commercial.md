## Commercial and multi-unit roofs

HOA-managed communities, apartment blocks, and commercial buildings, generally on
a planned cycle rather than a reactive one. On multi-building sites the useful
approach is usually to treat the whole community in one mobilisation — access is
already set up, and matched appearance across buildings is often the point for a
board.

Low-slope commercial roofs are a different job again, where drain clearance and
debris removal matter more than growth treatment.
