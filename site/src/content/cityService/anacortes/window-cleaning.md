A window in Anacortes usually faces one of two ways: toward the water, or toward
everything else. The first kind carries most of the grime, and the second tends
to stay clearer for longer. Pricing and scheduling glass here starts with
working out how much of the house falls into each group.

Soft tap water, a drier rain record than the mainland and a good number of
older houses complete the picture.

## What does the Anacortes setting do to window glass?

The city is surrounded by water on three sides, with Burrows Bay to the west,
Guemes Channel to the north and Fidalgo Bay to the east, and it counts more than
27 miles of shoreline. Many homes are built to face that water, and it is
usually the largest panes on the house that look out over it.

We have no measured salt-spray or wind data for Anacortes, so we will not tell
you how much salt lands on your glass. What we see across shoreline property in
general is that the elevation facing open water picks up a dull, even film that
dries on rather than running off in the rain, while sheltered sides mostly
collect ordinary dust, pollen and cobwebs. Treating those two surfaces as the
same job wastes time on the clean side and short-changes the exposed one.

Tree cover adds its own layer. The Community Forest Lands cover more than 2,950
acres inside the city, and glass on a house backing onto woodland tends to
collect sap specks, pollen and organic film in the frame corners.

## Does soft Anacortes water mean fewer spots?

Yes, it helps a good deal. Anacortes draws its water from the Skagit River,
treats it at a city plant near Mount Vernon and pipes it 14 miles to the Blue
Heron Reservoir. The 2025 Water Quality Report puts hardness in the 0 to 60
mg/L range, typically around 30 mg/L, and calls it soft.

That matters because the worst glass we deal with in hard-water areas is glass
where sprinkler overspray or a hose rinse has dried on, season after season,
until the mineral bonds to the surface. With soft water that process is much
slower. It is not absent: any rinse still carries dirt and dissolved material,
and a pane that dries in the sun after a hose-down will show it. We do not
rely on the tap either way. We bring our own water, filtered to remove what is
dissolved in it, so the glass dries clear without anything left on it.

## What is included in an Anacortes window visit?

The standard residential visit covers:

- **Exterior and interior glass** on every opening we can reach safely
- **Screens** taken out, washed, dried and put back in the same frame
- **Tracks and sills** vacuumed and wiped, since grit in a track wears the
  seals each time the sash slides
- **Frames** wiped where the finish allows it
- **Cobwebs** cleared from around the openings and the soffit just above
- **A final look round** with you, in daylight, before we load up

Skylights, glass deck rails, storm windows and sunroom roofs are quoted on
their own because access varies so much between houses. The full service hub
is at [window cleaning](/services/window-cleaning/).

## How is the glass actually cleaned?

By hand where a person can reach, and by pole where they cannot.

Ground-floor glass gets a cleaning solution, a scrubber to break up the film,
and a squeegee drawn across in one pass. On the water-facing elevation that
physical contact is what shifts the bonded film; rinsing alone leaves a haze.

Upper storeys, and panes over a deck, a roof or a planted bed, get a water-fed
pole carrying purified water. Water with its dissolved content filtered out
dries without leaving residue, so the glass can simply be left to dry. That
means no ladder on your decking and no squeegee pulled at arm's length on a
pane twenty feet up. Where a reach is honestly not safe, we say so on the quote
instead of improvising on the day.

## Do older Anacortes homes need different handling?

Often, yes. About 1,155 housing units in Anacortes, roughly 13% of the stock,
were built in 1939 or earlier, even though the median year built across the
city is 1989. The city keeps a Register of Historic Places that includes houses
such as the Wasserman-Lowman House on 8th Street and the Judd House on 9th
Street.

Older windows ask for more care than modern sealed units. Glazing putty can be
brittle, old glass can be thinner, and painted sashes do not like being soaked.
We work those windows by hand, keep blades away from old glass, and use less
water around the frames. If we spot a cracked pane, a loose light or a failed
seal on a newer unit, it goes on the walk-round list; failed seals are covered
on the [insulated glass units](/surface-library/insulated-glass-units/) page.
Pointing it out is as far as we go, since window repair is a different trade.

## What drives the price of window cleaning here?

Every quote is given on site. Five things decide it:

1. **Number of openings**, the starting point for any quote.
2. **Pane size.** A house with a wall of view glass facing the water can take
   longer than a smaller house with twice as many little panes.
3. **Height and access.** Ground-floor glass on a level lot is quick. Glass on
   a slope, over a deck, or three storeys up on a hillside takes longer.
4. **How long since the last clean.** A first visit to exposed glass that has
   gone several years is slower than a routine one, and later visits cost less.
5. **Additions** such as skylights, glass rails, storm windows and sunrooms.

A number given over the phone for a house nobody has seen tends to be wrong in
one direction or the other, which is why we would rather come and look.

## When should Anacortes windows be cleaned?

Spring is the main slot. The wettest months on the long Anacortes weather
record were November, at 3.84 inches, and December, at 3.80, and glass coming
out of that stretch carries rain spatter, organic film and the residue of
interior condensation. A clean in March or April means looking through clear
glass for the brighter half of the year.

Late summer or early autumn is the second slot, ahead of the wet months, so
the glass goes into the winter clean rather than already dulled.

If you are also having the roof or gutters done, book glass last. Roof and
gutter work sends debris and dirty water down the walls, and a crew that cleans
the windows first ends up doing them twice. The
[Anacortes roof cleaning page](/locations/anacortes/roof-cleaning/) and the
[Anacortes gutter page](/locations/anacortes/gutter-cleaning/) cover that
sequence.

## Where does the rinse water go?

Anacortes Municipal Code 18.30.050 allows only stormwater into the stormwater
system and names detergent among the illicit discharges. It also conditionally
permits routine external building wash-down that uses no detergent.

Window work fits that code comfortably when it is done properly. Pole work runs
plain purified water with nothing added. Ground-floor glass cleaned with a
solution is handled by hand, and the small amount that runs off is kept away
from street inlets and onto soil or lawn. The city's storm drains empty into
Fidalgo Bay, Guemes Channel and Burrows Bay, so we treat every inlet near the
house as a direct line to salt water. The wider approach is laid out under
[wash water and storm drains](/compliance/wash-water-and-storm-drains/).

## Can the water side be done on its own between full cleans?

Yes, and on a lot of Anacortes houses it is the most sensible way to spend the
money. If the view glass is where the film builds and the street side stays
presentable, there is little point paying for every opening three times a year.

A typical arrangement looks like this. A full visit in spring covers every
window inside and out, screens and tracks included. A shorter visit in late
summer covers only the exposed elevation, usually exterior only, with the pole
doing most of the work. Then a second full visit in autumn, if the house needs
it, or the following spring if it does not.

We will suggest that split on the walk-round when we think it fits, and we will
also say when it does not. A house tucked into trees, with no open outlook
over the water, may have no exposed side worth separating out at all. For
more on how the town's setting shapes the rest of the exterior, see the
[Anacortes overview](/locations/anacortes/).

## What window problems can cleaning not fix?

A few things look like dirt but are not, and it is better to hear it before
paying for a clean:

- **Fog inside double glazing** means a failed seal, and the haze is sealed in
  with it.
- **Etched glass** from years of dried-on deposit or chemical splash is damage
  to the surface itself and does not polish out with cleaning.
- **Scratches** from a previous blade or abrasive pad are permanent.
- **Paint and silicone** on glass need a separate careful job and are not part
  of a standard visit.

We mention any of these on the walk-round so the decision is yours.
