Everson windows get dirty on a farm calendar. Through the dry months, working
ground around the town puts fine dust into the air and it settles on every
pane facing the fields. Then the rain comes back, mixes with whatever is
already sitting on the glass, and leaves a grey film that lasts until spring.

That cycle is why a quick rinse rarely works here, and why we clean windows the
way we do.

## What makes Everson glass dirty?

Two seasons, each with its own kind of grime.

**The dry months bring dust.** The land around Everson and Nooksack grows
berries, carrots, potatoes and specialty produce, and supports dairy herds.
Farm work, trucks and tractors all lift fine soil into the air in dry weather.
On glass it lands as a thin, even layer that is easy to miss until the sun is
low and behind it.

**The wet months bring film.** Once autumn rain returns, it catches that dust,
along with pollen, road spray and organic debris from trees and gutters, and
dries it into a smeared, patchy layer. North-facing panes and glass under deep
eaves are worst, because they stay damp longest.

Traffic is the third, quieter source. Everson and Nooksack sit on SR 544 and
SR 9, and houses close to a busy road carry an oily road film on the glass that
faces it. It does not come off with water alone.

## Why do we bring our own water?

Because we do not know what is in yours, and neither does the published
record. Everson's drinking water comes from the Strandell Well Field, about
three-quarters of a mile southwest of downtown. The main source, Deep Well #6,
draws from an aquifer 156 feet down, and two shallower wells are held in
reserve. The city's water report lists plenty about the supply, but it gives
no figure for hardness.

That leaves two options: assume the tap water is fine to dry on glass, or take
the question off the table. We take it off the table. For water-fed pole work,
where glass is rinsed and left to dry on its own, we use purified water we carry
with us. Water with the dissolved minerals removed dries clear, so the pane has
nothing left on it once the last drop is gone.

For squeegee work on reachable glass, the water is wiped off rather than left to
dry, so the source matters much less.

## What is included in an Everson window clean?

A standard inside-and-outside visit covers:

- **Every accessible pane**, inside and outside
- **Screens** taken out, washed, dried and put back in the same openings
- **Tracks** vacuumed and wiped, so grit is not ground into the runners
- **Sills and frames** wiped down wherever they are washable
- **Sliding and French doors** as part of the glass count
- **A final walk-round** with you, checking panes in good light

Outside-only cleans are available if you prefer to handle the inside yourself.
Skylights, conservatory roofs and very large feature glass are quoted
separately, as access for those varies widely from house to house.

## How is the work done?

It depends on the pane, not the house.

### Reachable glass: scrub and squeegee

Ground-floor windows and anything within safe reach get the traditional method.
A cleaning solution goes on with a scrubber that loosens the film, then a
squeegee pulls it off in one clean stroke, and the edges are detailed by hand.
Nothing beats this for removing bonded grime, and it is fast.

### Upper and awkward glass: water-fed pole

Higher windows, and glass over decks, planting or steep ground, are cleaned
from the ground using a telescopic pole with a soft brush head fed with
purified water. The brush agitates the dirt, the water rinses it away, and the
glass dries without spots. There is no ladder leaning on the siding and no one
standing in your flower beds.

### When a window needs something more

Some marks do not come off with a normal clean: paint flecks, adhesive from
labels, mortar splash on newer builds, and mineral staining that has sat on the
glass for years. We tell you what we see on the quote, and we will not take a
blade to glass where there is any risk of scratching it.

## What about double glazing?

A large share of Everson's housing dates from the 1970s onward, and many houses of that age now have double-glazed windows, either original or fitted later. A sealed unit has two panes with
a gap between them. We clean both outer faces, but we cannot reach the inside of
the gap.

If a window looks cloudy or misted and the haze does not move when you wipe
either side, the seal has failed and moisture has got in. That is a glazing
repair, not a cleaning problem. Our
[insulated glass units](/surface-library/insulated-glass-units/) profile
explains how that happens and what the options are.

## Do older and newer houses need different care?

Yes, and Everson has both ends of the range. The census counts 105 homes built
in 1939 or earlier and 77 built since 2020, and the glass in those two groups
behaves very differently under a squeegee.

### Older windows

A pre-war house may still have wooden sash windows, sometimes with single
panes held in with glazing putty. Old putty can be dry and cracked, and old
glass is thinner and less forgiving than modern units. We use lighter pressure
on the blade, keep solution away from failing putty and bare wood, and tell you
where a sash is sticking or a pane is loose rather than forcing it. Storm
windows fitted over older sashes are cleaned on both faces where they come out,
and on the outside only where they are fixed.

### Newer windows

A new build brings the opposite problems. Glass fresh from construction often
carries stickers, adhesive, paint overspray, stucco or mortar splash and a
layer of fine dust from the site. Modern glass may also carry coatings that
should not be scraped. We treat a first clean on a new home as its own job,
work slowly around the frames and any coating, and quote it separately from a
routine visit.

### Everything in between

The 1970s and 1980s homes, the largest share of the town, often have aluminium or vinyl frames, and many have had replacement windows fitted since.
The tracks on older sliders are the usual weak point: grit wears the rollers,
and a clean track makes a stiff window noticeably easier to open.

## What drives the cost here?

Pricing is done on site. The main factors are:

1. **Number of panes and their size**, which is the starting point
2. **Storeys and access**, since upper glass over obstacles takes longer
3. **Inside as well as outside**, which roughly means two jobs in one visit
4. **Screens, tracks and doors**, and how many of them there are
5. **Condition**, especially if the glass has not been cleaned in years
6. **Specialist work** such as post-construction cleaning or skylights

A home that is cleaned regularly costs less per visit than one that has gone
several years between cleans, because we are maintaining rather than
restoring. Details of the service as a whole are on our main
[window cleaning](/services/window-cleaning/) page.

## When should Everson windows be booked?

Spring and early autumn are the two natural windows in Everson.

A spring clean, once the worst of the rain has passed, removes the winter film
and lets you enjoy the long evenings through clear glass. An early-autumn clean,
after the dry months and before the heavy rain, clears the dust that summer left
behind. Booking before the dust has settled means booking again soon after.

Selling a house or changing tenants is another good moment. More than a third of Everson's occupied homes are rented, 369 of 988, and clean glass is one of the
cheapest ways to make an empty house look cared for when it goes back on the
market.

We avoid cleaning in hard frost, as water freezing on the glass leaves a worse
result and makes ladders unsafe.

## Where does the water go?

Very little of it goes anywhere. Squeegee work uses a small amount of solution
that is wiped off, and pole work uses purified water that runs down the siding
and into the ground. Neither involves heavy detergent or a lot of runoff.

We could not verify Everson's own code on wash water or storm drains, so we
make no claim about what it allows. Rules vary between jurisdictions around the
county, and our
[wash water and storm drains](/compliance/wash-water-and-storm-drains/) page
covers how we handle runoff on bigger jobs and cites the codes we have read.

## What goes well with a window clean?

A [house wash](/locations/everson/house-washing/), mostly. Soft washing the
siding sends rinse water down over the windows, so washing the walls first and
the glass last means you only pay for the glass once. The same is true of roofs
and gutters: anything that sheds debris onto the windows should be done before
them.

so two addresses can often be booked for the same day.
