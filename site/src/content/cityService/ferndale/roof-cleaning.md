A roof in Ferndale spends most of the cool half of the year wet, and whatever
side of it faces away from the sun stays wet longest. That is where the growth
starts: black streaks first, then moss in the gaps, then the crusty patches of
lichen that are the hardest of the three to shift. The good news is that a lot of
Ferndale roofs are young enough to catch it early.

This page covers how we clean roofs in Ferndale, why we never use pressure, and
what drives the timing and the price.

## What is growing on Ferndale roofs?

Three different organisms, which look similar from the driveway and behave very
differently up close.

**Algae** shows as dark vertical streaks running down from the ridge, most visible
on lighter shingles. It is the most cosmetic of the three and the quickest to
disappear once treated.

**Moss** grows in the joints between shingles and along the lower edge, where
water lingers longest. It holds moisture against the roof, lifts shingle edges as
it thickens, and sheds debris into the gutters below.

**Lichen** forms flat, pale green or grey patches that grip the surface itself.
Treated lichen dies quickly and lets go slowly, sometimes over a full year, and
trying to remove it by force takes roof with it.

On a typical house the growth concentrates on the north-facing slope, under
any overhanging tree, and along the bottom rows above a gutter that has been
holding water. A roof on open ground with good sun on every face can go years with
very little, while the same roof in shade can need attention every couple of
years.

## Why does a young Ferndale roof deserve care?

Because it has the most to lose.

The census puts the median year built for Ferndale's housing at 1998, and 1,690 of
the town's roughly 6,200 homes went up in 2010 or later. A large share of the
town's roofs are therefore likely still on their first covering, and on a
composition roof that means the granule layer is mostly intact.

Those granules are the shingle's sun protection. Once they are gone, the asphalt
underneath weathers much faster. A pressure washer can strip years of that
protection in an afternoon, and nothing puts it back. Soft washing leaves the
granules where they are. The
[composition asphalt shingle](/surface-library/composition-asphalt-shingle/)
profile explains how that surface is built and how it ages.

On an older house with a roof that has already lost much of its granule layer, the
same approach applies with even more care, because the shingle has less margin left.

## What about metal and manufactured-home roofs?

Ferndale counts 411 mobile homes in the census figures, and their roofs are a
separate case. Many are low-pitched, some are metal, and not all of them are built
to carry a person walking on them. Before quoting we look at how the roof is
built and decide how it can be reached. Often that means working from ladders and
the edge rather than from the roof itself.

Metal roofs, whether on a manufactured home, a barn or a newer house, are soft
washed too. The coating on a metal panel is its protection in the same way granules
are on a shingle, and it does not take kindly to force or to abrasive scrubbing.
The [standing seam metal roof](/surface-library/standing-seam-metal-roof/) profile
goes into that surface in more detail.

## What does a Ferndale roof clean include?

A standard roof visit covers:

1. **A look at the roof before anything goes on it,** to identify the material,
   the growth and any obvious damage we should mention.
2. **Planting and surfaces below protected,** with beds soaked in plain water and
   delicate items covered or moved.
3. **Soft-wash treatment of every affected face,** at low pressure, with a solution
   matched to the roofing and the growth.
4. **Loose moss lifted by hand** where it has built into thick mats, without
   scraping the shingle surface.
5. **A gentle rinse** of the roof, the walls it drained down and the ground below.
6. **Gutters checked** for anything the treatment has washed into them.
7. **A note on anything we noticed,** such as lifted shingles or damaged flashing,
   for you to follow up with a roofer.

## How does soft washing work on a Ferndale roof?

It swaps force for time. The solution goes on at roughly the pressure of a garden
hose and stays on long enough to kill what is growing. After that, the job
continues without us: over the next few weeks, rain and wind carry off the dead
growth, and the roof keeps improving after the truck has left.

That delay is worth understanding before booking. Algae streaks usually fade within
days. Moss turns brown and dry and lets go over several weeks. Lichen can take a
season or more. Anyone who promises a spotless roof on the day is either scraping
or blasting, and both take shingle with them.

We keep our weight off fragile roofing wherever we can, work from ladders or a
walkable ridge where the roof allows it, and avoid treating in direct hot sun or
ahead of heavy rain, when the solution would either dry too quickly or wash away
before it has done its work. The general method, and why pressure never has a
place on a roof, is laid out on the main [roof cleaning](/services/roof-cleaning/)
page.

## What if the moss has got under the shingles?

Then the roof needs more than a surface clean. Moss that has worked its way under
the shingle edges lifts them, lets wind-driven rain in behind them and keeps the
underside wet. You can often spot it from the ground as shingle edges that no
longer sit flat along the lower rows.

The approach is still gentle: treat the moss so it dies, lift the loose material
out from under the edges by hand, and let the rest weather away. The page on
[moss under shingles](/services/moss-removal/moss-under-shingles/) covers what to
look for and why forcing it out does more harm than good. If lifted shingles have
cracked or lost their seal, that is work for a roofer, and we will tell you so.

## What drives the cost of roof cleaning in Ferndale?

The price is set on site after we have seen the roof, and it depends on:

- **Roof area,** the starting point of any quote.
- **Pitch and height.** A steep two-storey roof needs more setup and slower work
  than a low single-storey one.
- **Material.** Composition, metal and older or fragile roofing each need a
  slightly different approach.
- **How established the growth is.** Light algae on a young roof is a quick job.
  Thick moss and old lichen take more treatment and more hand work.
- **Access and surroundings.** Planting close to the eaves, conservatories, decks
  and tight side yards all add protection work.
- **Runoff management,** which depends on where the downspouts go and how close the
  nearest drain is.

Roof and gutters booked together share one visit, and the order is always roof
first.

## When should a Ferndale roof be cleaned?

Treatment works well from spring into early autumn, during a spell of mild, dry
weather. The solution needs a few dry hours to work, and the dead growth comes off
faster when it is followed by ordinary rain rather than a downpour.

The time to look at your roof, though, is the wet season. Moss is swollen and
bright green in winter and shrunken and grey by late summer, so a roof judged in
August almost always looks healthier than it is. If you have been meaning to get
the roof seen to, a quote in winter and treatment in spring is a sensible rhythm.

## Where does the rinse go, and what do Ferndale's rules say?

This is where roof work differs most from a plain rinse. Inside city limits,
Ferndale Municipal Code 13.35.020(B)(4) allows "routine external building wash
down that does not use detergents." A roof soft wash uses a cleaning solution, so
it sits outside that allowance and falls back under the code's general
prohibition on other discharges.

In practice that means planning around the gutters, because a roof drains straight
into them. Where downspouts feed a storm connection, we manage them so the first
runoff from the treatment does not go down that pipe. Nearby street inlets get
covered, and the rinse is sent onto lawn and planting rather than into a drain.

In the unincorporated Ferndale Urban Growth Area, the county's illicit-discharge
rules under WCC chapter 16.36 apply instead, because Whatcom County's 2025
Stormwater Management Program includes the UGA in its permit area. The wider
comparison is on the
[wash water and storm drains](/compliance/wash-water-and-storm-drains/) page.

## Should the gutters be done at the same time?

Almost always. A roof clean sends moss, granules and dead growth down into the
gutters, and if the gutters were already part-full, that is enough to block an
outlet. Clearing them straight after the roof means the next heavy rain has a clean
path to the ground. How we handle that part of the job is on
[Ferndale gutter cleaning](/locations/ferndale/gutter-cleaning/).
