the job.

Salt overspray had been landing on the exterior glass and drying there for a long
time. Spiders had been busy around the frames. Between the two, the glass and the
green frames had picked up enough buildup to noticeably dull the thing the house
was designed around — the view.

## What the exposure had done

Waterfront properties get a harsher deal than homes a few miles inland, and the
glass is where it shows first.

Wind off the water carries fine saltwater droplets onto the panes. The moisture
evaporates; the salt does not. It builds in layers, and because each layer is
deposited by drying rather than by dust settling, it streaks rather than films.
That is the mineral streaking visible in the before photographs.

Around the frames the problem was different. Spider activity had left droppings
and webbing along the channels, in the corners and under the sills — the places
that never see direct rain and never self-clean.

## How we cleaned it

- **Agitated the surfaces first.** Salt deposits and spider debris are physically
  attached to the glass and frames. They come off by being worked loose, not by
  being rinsed at.
- **Cleaned the frames as well as the glass**, including the channels and corners
  where the webbing had collected.
- **Rinsed with purified water.** The minerals are filtered out of the water
  before it reaches the pane, so once the glass is properly clean and thoroughly
  rinsed it can dry on its own without spotting.
- **Reached the upper glass from the ground** on a water-fed pole, rather than
  putting ladders into the planting beds and the decking.

## Why the frames mattered as much as the glass

This is the part that separated the finished result from a quick clean.

If the debris around the frames stays put, it does not stay put for long. The
first rain moves it back onto the glass, and the pane that looked clean at
handover looks streaked again within a week. Cleaning the frames is what makes
the glass stay clean long enough to be worth paying for.

The close-up frames in the before-and-after photographs are the clearest evidence
on this page. The glass is the obvious difference; the frames are the reason it
holds.

## What it looked like afterwards

The heavy contamination came off the exterior glass, the green frames were
noticeably cleaner, and the water and islands were back to being what you look at
through these windows.

On a waterfront property that is the real deliverable. Clean windows here are not
an appearance upgrade to the house — they are the difference between having the
view you paid for and looking at a haze.

Salt exposure is heaviest right on the water, but moisture, pollen and insect
